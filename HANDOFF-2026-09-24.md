## Realtime issue: diagnosis and fix

The user reported broken Low latency mode and truncated or missing words. The pinned upstream realtime engine returned audio delayed by `extra_time_right`, which was effectively 2 seconds because our wrapper had not overridden its default. Its VAD gate used **current input activity** to mute that delayed converted output. A short phrase could therefore be completely silenced even after successful inference. The old Low latency block was 120 ms with 6 diffusion steps and little processing margin.

Commit `5bc2830` changes:

- `worker/realtime/engine.py`: explicitly sets right context and diffusion steps per preset: Low latency 120 ms / 5 steps / 120 ms right context; Balanced 240 ms / 8 / 240 ms; Quality 400 ms / 12 / 400 ms.
- `worker/realtime/patch_upstream.py` and `Dockerfile`: apply a checked patch to the pinned upstream fork at image build. It removes the mismatched VAD output gate, its per-block computation, and unused VAD model load. The script fails the build if the expected upstream text changes.
- `worker/realtime/service.py` and `desktop/src/api.ts`: expose `lookahead_seconds` in runtime stats and WebRTC answer.
- `desktop/src/pages/RealtimePage.tsx`: when Stop is clicked, keep the WebRTC track alive with silence for at least 700 ms, allowing the final word through the right context and playout buffer; display “Finishing last words…”. The displayed latency estimate includes lookahead.
- `worker/realtime/short_utterance_test.py`: GPU smoke test using a 0.72-second excerpt from the pinned upstream sample. It checks converted output activity and onset for all three presets.

Baseline on the worker: the old Low latency setup produced silence for the short excerpt (`peak=0`, no active blocks). The updated GPU test passed on `cuda:0`:

| Preset | Audio block | Mean processing | p95 processing | Converted active output | Peak RMS |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Low latency | 120 ms | 83 ms | 83 ms | 0.84 s | 0.419 |
| Balanced | 240 ms | 114 ms | 115 ms | 0.96 s | 0.315 |
| Quality | 400 ms | 157 ms | 160 ms | 0.80 s | 0.293 |

This validates model processing and preservation of a short sample; it does **not** replace a live microphone/WebRTC/virtual-cable listening test, especially for final-word behavior after Stop or for network jitter.
